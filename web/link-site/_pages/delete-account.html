In the app

  1. Open RoadWise and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account. If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, confirm with Apple when it asks.

Want a copy first? Tap Export my data on the same screen before you delete.

By email

If you can't use the app, email roadwise.contact@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to confirm it's yours.

What gets deleted

Your account, profile, username, drives, routes, driving events, scores, goals, achievements, friends, friend requests, blocks, reports you made, Wrapped links, devices and push tokens are deleted from our servers right away. If you signed in with Apple, RoadWise's access to your Apple ID is revoked. If you signed in with Google, you can remove RoadWise from your Google Account settings. The app then erases your RoadWise data from the phone.

What we keep

Reports other people filed about your account may be kept to protect the community. Data that isn't linked to any account, such as speed-limit map data, stays as our Privacy Policy describes. Copies can remain in our providers' logs and backups for a limited time. Signing in again later starts a new, empty account.

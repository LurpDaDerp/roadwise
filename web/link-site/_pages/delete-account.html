Delete it in the app

  1. Open RoadWise and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Select Account, and then select Delete account.
  3. Enter DELETE, and then select Delete account.

If you signed in with Apple on an iPhone, Apple asks you to confirm.

Want a copy of your data first? Select Export your data on the same screen before you delete.

Delete it by email

Can't use the app? Email roadwise.contact@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We may ask you to prove the account is yours.

What we delete

We delete these from our servers right away:

  • Your account, profile and username
  • Your drives, routes, driving events and scores
  • Your goals, achievements and Wrapped links
  • Your friends, friend requests, blocks and the reports you made
  • Your devices and notification tokens

Then the app erases your RoadWise data from your phone. If you signed in with Apple and delete on an iPhone, we also ask Apple to remove RoadWise's access to your Apple ID. Otherwise, you can remove RoadWise in your Apple ID or Google Account settings.

What we keep

  • Reports other people made about your account, to keep the community safe.
  • Data that isn't linked to any account, like speed limit map data. Our Privacy Policy explains this.
  • Copies in our providers' logs and backups, for a limited time.

If you sign in again later, you start with a new, empty account.

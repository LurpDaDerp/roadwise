Drives don't start on their own

Automatic drives need these settings for RoadWise:

  • iPhone: Location set to Always with Precise Location on, and Motion & Fitness on. Background App Refresh helps iOS wake RoadWise; Low Power Mode turns it off.
  • Android: Location set to "Allow all the time" with precise location, and Physical activity allowed. Battery usage for RoadWise must not be Restricted, and the phone needs Google Play services.

RoadWise lists anything missing under Profile › Settings › Permissions. To be sure a drive records, swipe Start drive on Home before you pull out.

A drive was recorded when I wasn't driving

Tap "Not driving?" on the driving screen while it shows, and the drive is discarded. After a drive, its recap offers "Wasn't driving" for 24 hours, twice a month, so it won't count toward your Safety Rating.

An event looks wrong

Open the drive's recap and tap Report under the event. You can report an event for 7 days after the drive.

DashPal

Pair your DashPal dashcam in Profile › Settings › DashPal. Keep Bluetooth on so its alerts play and its events join your drives.

Your data

To get a copy of your data, open the Profile tab, tap Account, then Export my data. To delete your account and its data, see Delete your account.

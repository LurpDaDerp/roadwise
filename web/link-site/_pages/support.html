Why don't my drives start on their own?

RoadWise needs a few settings to record drives by itself.

  • iPhone: Set Location to Always and turn on Precise Location and Motion & Fitness. Background App Refresh helps too. Low Power Mode turns it off.
  • Android: Set Location to "Allow all the time" with precise location, and allow Physical activity. Don't set RoadWise's battery use to Restricted. Your phone also needs Google Play services.

To see what's missing, go to Profile › Settings › Permissions. To be sure a drive records, swipe Start drive on Home before you go.

RoadWise recorded a drive, but I wasn't driving

As the drive starts, tap Not driving? on the driving screen. It's there for your first 2 minutes on that screen. RoadWise throws the drive away.

After the drive, open its recap and tap Wasn't driving. You can do this within 24 hours, twice a month. The drive then won't count toward your Safety Rating.

An event looks wrong

Open the drive's recap and tap Report under the event. Most events can be reported for 7 days after the drive. A hard start or sharp turn can only be reported as Wasn't driving, within 24 hours.

How do I use my DashPal?

Pair it in Profile › Settings › DashPal. Keep Bluetooth on so its alerts play and its events show up in your drives.

How do I get or delete my data?

To get a copy, go to the Profile tab, select Account, and then select Export your data. To delete your account, see Delete your account.

While you drive

  • Starts on its own. Set location to Always and RoadWise records your drives by itself, even when the app is closed. To be sure, swipe Start drive before you go.
  • A simple driving screen. Your speed, the speed limit, and one tap to your maps app.
  • Alerts you hear, not read. With RoadWise open, a sound and a buzz tell you when you speed, brake hard or pick up your phone. Touch controls lock at speed.
  • Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. See your speed and the limit without opening the app. On Android, it's a drive notification.
  • Weather warnings. A heads-up about ice, heavy rain, dense fog and other bad weather near you.
  • Break reminders. After two hours on the road, it reminds you to rest at your next stop.
  • Emergency button. One tap opens your phone to call 911 or a contact you saved.

After every drive

  • A score out of 100. Every drive gets one, along with its distance and time.
  • See what happened, and where. Hard brakes, hard starts, sharp turns, speeding and phone pickups are listed and pinned on your route.
  • Report a mistake. Think RoadWise got something wrong? Tap Report under the event.
  • Riding, not driving? Tap Not driving? as the drive starts, or Wasn't driving afterward, and it won't count.
  • Share your drive. A card with your route and score, ready to post.

Track your progress

  • Safety Rating. One number from 0 to 100 for your last 90 days. It's fair: driving more doesn't raise it, driving well does.
  • Six scores. Speed, braking, acceleration, turning, phone use, and attention with a DashPal dashcam.
  • Spots to watch. Places where the same slip keeps happening, with a heads-up as you drive toward one.
  • Weekly goals and badges. Three goals each week and achievements to unlock.
  • Monthly Wrapped. A story of your month on the road that you can share.

Drive with friends

  • Friends board. See how your Safety Rating compares with your friends'.
  • Add friends fast. Search a username or scan a friend's QR code.
  • You choose what they see. Share your rating, scores and drive map, or keep them to yourself.

Made for new drivers

  • Permit practice log. Counts your supervised hours, day and night, and makes a PDF of your log.
  • Your places. Save Home, Work and School. Drives get names, and you get a commute card and one-tap directions.
  • Your car. Pair your car's Bluetooth or CarPlay. If a drive starts without it, RoadWise asks if you're the one driving.
  • Where you parked. RoadWise remembers where you left the car and can walk you back.
  • DashPal dashcam. Pair a DashPal and it warns you about drowsiness and looking away from the road.

Private by default

  • Trimmed routes. The start and end of every route are cut before it leaves your phone.
  • Privacy zones. Hide your routes near places you choose.
  • On your phone only. Your saved places and emergency contacts never leave your phone.
  • Never sold. Nothing is sold, and nothing goes to insurers.
  • Your data, your call. Export it or delete your account at any time.

Screens on this page are examples. RoadWise is a coaching aid and can miss or misread events. Keep your eyes on the road.
